# Football fashion, late 2026, and what MatchTracker should wear

Researched 24 November 2026. Read against `DESIGN.md`, the current phone screenshots (dark, light, indoor, team, share) and the watch captures.

**Recommendation: Direction A, "Woven".** The heat map becomes cloth. Numbers become kit numbers. System green and black give way to bottle green, ivory and one copper trim. Borrow the poster layout from Direction B for the share card only.

How sources are marked:

- **[P]** page fetched and read
- **[I]** image looked at directly
- **[S]** search excerpt only, so weaker

Nothing below proposes using a club, brand or federation mark. Names appear only as evidence.

---

## 1. Why the app reads as "bland iOS" today

Seen in the screenshots:

1. **The palette is Apple's.** Accent is exactly `#30D158`, loss `#FF453A`, card yellow `#FFD60A`, sprint `#FF9F0A`, on pure black and `systemGroupedBackground`. Any stock Fitness-style app has these.
2. **The type is the default.** SF Pro Bold titles and SF Rounded numerals, the Activity/Fitness voice.
3. **Every container is one rounded grey rectangle.** Summary, Matches, Personal bests and All numbers are the same inset-grouped list.
4. **The one original thing, the heat map, is a blurred green glow.** That is the generic look for heat. The app icon already does something better: hard contour bands.
5. **Light mode has no identity outside the hero.** White on `#F2F2F7` with green links.

Two assets worth keeping. The season wall already looks like a rack of shirts, and the indoor stint thumbnails already look like hooped jerseys.

## 4. Platform facts that bound all three

- **SF widths on iPhone: yes.** `Font.width` is iOS 16+ and works with text styles (`.largeTitle.width(.expanded)`), so Dynamic Type and Bold Text keep working. SF Rounded has **no width axis**, so compressed kit numerals mean leaving SF Rounded for the hero numbers.
- **SF widths on Apple Watch: do not assume.** `Font.width` compiles on watchOS 9+, but `SFCompact.ttf` and `SFCompactRounded.ttf` in the watchOS 26.5 simulator runtime have no `wdth` axis (inspected). That runtime also ships `SFUI.ttf`, which has one; whether the system resolves to it, and on watchOS 10, is untested. Plan on a bundled OFL font or plain SF Compact there.
- **SwiftUI shaders** (`colorEffect`, `layerEffect`) are iOS 17+ and **not on watchOS**. Use `Canvas` for anything shared.
- **Prototyped** for this report with SwiftUI `Canvas` on macOS at 2x: stitch heat, halftone heat and line heat all stay recognisable down to 44 x 66 pt. Numerals tested too:
  - A straight cut through the **middle reads as strikethrough** at 28 pt and slices the score dash. Rejected.
  - A cut at **62% of the line height** reads as a kit number from 44 pt up. Good.
  - A per-digit 28 degree diagonal cut also reads. Second choice.
  - Punching an ultra-light glyph out of a black one **misregisters badly**. Rejected.
- Renders are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ben-Development-MatchTracker--claude-worktrees-heuristic-meitner-343b2f/b7ef35b9-cace-4905-bd51-1d1861fdb9c9/scratchpad/ff/p.png` and `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-ben-Development-MatchTracker--claude-worktrees-heuristic-meitner-343b2f/b7ef35b9-cace-4905-bd51-1d1861fdb9c9/scratchpad/ff/q.png` (temporary).

**Legal guard rails for every direction.** Trim is one or two bands, **never three parallel stripes** on a shoulder, sleeve or side. No red and white checkerboard, no clover or leaf marks, no shields with initials, no crest shapes at all. No supplier-style jock tag. Kit patterns are never named after a club. No tournament names.

## 5. Direction A: Woven (recommended)

**One line:** every match is knitted into its own cloth, and the numbers on it are kit numbers.

**Why now:** jacquard and tonal weave are where this season's admired shirts put their effort (2, 3, 4, 5). Nike turned heat maps into knit (8), so the metaphor is how kits are really made. One-of-one shirts are news (11). Cut, narrow numerals are the year's shared typographic idea (14-17, 20). Green, ivory, claret and copper run from World Cup to terrace tailoring (2, 5, 6, 7, 16, 31).

Win, draw and loss are also told apart by shape and word: filled, outlined, outlined with a bar. Heat ramp, five yarns: `#0B3B2C`, `#145740`, `#2E7D55`, `#8DBB72`, `#F5EEDD`. Neighbouring steps are only 1.5 to 2.3 apart by design; the hot end is 10.8 against cloth, and rule 11's sentence carries the meaning. With Increase Contrast, drop to three yarns (first, third, last).

**Type**
- Screen titles and field names: SF Pro **Expanded**, heavy, sentence case, through text styles.
- Hero numerals and score: SF Pro **Compressed**, heavy, tabular, with the cut at 62% of line height, stroke 3.5% of size (minimum 1.5 pt). **Only at 44 pt and above.** Never on the dash, point or units.
- Row values: SF Pro Condensed semibold, no cut.
- Everything else: SF Pro text styles.
- Watch: SF Compact heavy, or Sofia Sans Extra Condensed if it measures larger at the same width. No cut on the live clock.
- Numerals look like kit numbers: **yes on heroes, score, share and wall captions; no in lists, forms, charts and live watch metrics.**

**Pattern** (all `Canvas`, no images)
- *Stitch:* V-shaped knit stitch on a grid, width `w`, row pitch `0.8w`; color is the heat value quantized to five yarns. `w` is about 1/28 of pitch width, floor 3 pt. Batch into one `Path` per yarn (five fills), rasterize thumbnails once.
- *Pinstripe:* 1 pt ivory at 13% every 18 pt on cloth. Ivory text directly over a stripe is 7.6:1.
- *Trim:* two bands at the hem, ivory 4 pt, gap 3 pt, copper 4 pt.
- *Hoops:* the indoor stints as a hooped shirt.
- Pattern by kind of match: pitch match, stitch; indoor, hoops; no location, pinstripe. Behind text, pattern stays at 1.5:1 or less against cloth (pinstripe 1.4, first yarn 1.5); hotter yarns never sit under text.

**Shape:** 22 pt continuous corners on cloth, square inner pitch, flat hem bands flush to the bottom edge. No strokes around cards.

**Motion:** replay *knits*: stitches fill along the player's path. Hero weave draws in once, row by row, 0.6 s. Cut lines slide 2 pt apart on a goal. Reduce Motion: cross-fade.

| Dark and light parity | Strong | Light-led | Dark-led |

| Uses the app's own data | Best (heat is the cloth) | Good | Good |
| Icon harmony | Best | Weak | Medium |
| HIG and web-look risk | Low | Higher | Low |

**Choose A.** Confidence: about 80% that it is the right direction, 90% that the palette and numeral change alone removes "bland iOS", 65% that stitch beats blur for this owner until he sees it on a phone in daylight.

### Change first, in this order

1. **Tokens.** Replace accent, canvas, surface, text, loss, draw and heart in `iOS App/Theme.swift`, `iOS Shared/SurfacePalette.swift` and `Watch App/WatchTheme.swift` with A's table. Largest visible change per line. While there, fix drift: `Theme.turf` and `SurfacePalette.us` have different light values, and `heart` differs in dark.
2. **Numerals and titles.** `HeroNumeral` from SF Rounded to SF Pro Compressed heavy with the 62% cut at 44 pt and up; hero, match and share titles to `.width(.expanded)`.
3. **The weave.** One `WovenHeat` `Canvas` behind the existing heat API, used by the Summary hero, match pitch, season wall, list swatch and share card. Cloth plus hem trim on the hero.
4. **Season wall as rack.** 2 pt gaps, hem bands for results, hoops for indoor.
5. **Share card** on B's poster layout with A's cloth.
6. **Watch end screen** band and cut result. Live glance changes color tokens only.
7. Later, optional: *your colors*. The player picks cloth, ivory substitute and trim from pre-checked sets (data 11, 39, 41, 42). Never free color, never named after clubs; clamp to the ratios above.

Steps 1 and 2 are a day's work and answer the complaint alone. Judge step 3 on device in sun before spreading it.

### Leave native

Navigation and tab bars, Liquid Glass, search, back, share and menu buttons, segmented pickers, sliders, sheets, forms, All numbers, Edit match, settings, paywall buttons, date pickers, alerts, context menus, comments, charts' axes, system fonts for body. No custom tab icons, no textured rows, no patterned sheets.

### `DESIGN.md` amendments this needs

- **Rule 3:** text styles with `.width` allowed. Kit numerals (Compressed heavy, cut at 62%) at 44 pt or larger; SF Rounded retires from iPhone heroes.
- **Rule 4:** unchanged. Kit lettering is uppercase on shirts; stay sentence case.
- **Rule 5:** add "One cloth surface per screen. Cloth has no stroke, no nested card; trim is one or two bands."
- **Rule 6:** add a fourth job: copper marks a best or the match you are in, always with a word or symbol. Draw gets its own neutral.
- **New rule:** patterns are made of match data or kind of match, stay at 1.5:1 or less behind text, and vanish with Reduce Transparency or at accessibility sizes where they meet text.
- **Rule 11:** woven heat keeps its sentence.

### Acceptance checks

- Accessibility 5 with the hero stacked; cut numerals shrink to fit, cut removed below 44 pt rendered size.
- VoiceOver reads the score as one string; decoration hidden.
- Increase Contrast, Differentiate Without Color, Bold Text, Reduce Motion, Smart Invert, grayscale.
- Stitch at 44 x 66 on 2x and 3x, and in Always On, at arm's length in sun.
- Season wall scroll stays smooth with 35 cached swatches.
- Deuteranopia: win and loss separable by hem shape alone.
- Legal pass: no three-stripe trim, no crest shapes, no club-like default pairing.

## 9. What could not be verified

- **Stitch legibility and performance on a device.** Only macOS `Canvas` renders with synthetic heat.
- **SF width on watchOS.** SF Compact lacks the axis in the 26.5 simulator; behavior on watchOS 10 and whether the system substitutes SF Pro are untested. Tabular digits in Sofia Sans Extra Condensed not confirmed.
- **NWSL patterns and colors.** Pages gave no descriptions and images were not inspected. The independent women's and grassroots-label field is thin here; only a vendor blog (42).
- **Independent magazines.** One 2026 issue found (30). Mundial and SEASON 2026 work not found, nor a 2026 independent-label survey.
- **Indoor and futsal as fashion.** Product pages and revivals, no dated trend analysis. Scarves and tickets: pre-2026 fashion coverage only, so nothing rests on them.
- **Kit colors.** Article text or photos, not supplier color codes. All hex values here are this report's own.
- **Fashion sources are partly marketing.** Brand and retailer pieces (31, 33, 34, 42) describe what they sell. Footy Headlines mixes releases and leaks; items used are marked released or seen. Its Liverpool (17) and boot (36) pages carry conflicting dates.
- **Post-World Cup demand.** No sales data for November 2026. Trend strength is inferred from frequency across sources.
- **Fast Company on Rocastle** refused fetches; SoccerBible was used instead.
